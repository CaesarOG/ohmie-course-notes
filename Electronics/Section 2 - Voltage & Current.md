# Voltage (the push)
A water tower doesn’t throw water, just holds it high, potential difference of energy in height does the pushing. Batteries play same game with charge separation. Electron sea left alone never motion together, jiggling never adding up to a march. No source, no voltage potential difference gap, no push. Something has to push. Voltage potential difference then drives the crowd. A battery for example its chemistry keeps one end crowded with charge electrons, other end lacking it positive, and holds that difference, this difference creates the push.

Potential difference, energy waiting for every unit of charge that makes the trip. Stack more chemistry, hold bigger difference, push harder.

Volts are the unit of voltage / potential difference.

With the water tower analogy, charge doesn’t get used up like water pouring out, potential just moves the sea of charge along in motion. Electrons push out of the negative terminal and are pulled into the positive terminal. Voltage is the potential energy itself, the potential difference that would drive charge if given a medium a wire in closed circuit, complete path. Pressure, waiting for pipe. No flow without complete path and battery still holds full difference.

# Current (the flow)
The rate or busyness of the motion is current. Just like standing beside a road and counting the cars passing each second to know how busy. Do the same for charge and you know the current. If charge in motion down the wire, push potential difference is working, but how much charge is actually passing is it a trickle or a flood, so current measures the rate of motion. Charge moving through a section of wire per second, more per second more current.

Amperes are the unit of current / flow.

That per second count measure being amperes, one amp is actually already somewhat enormous in that six billion billion (6.2 quintillion) electrons a.k.a one coulomb of charge per second are drifting/moving. One phone charger is about 2 amps. Voltage is the push/potential diff, waiting. Current is the flow/count happening. Less resistance given same voltage, gives higher current as less fighting.

# Conventional vs. Electric Current
By 1700s everyone agreed something flows around working circuit. But nobody could see what, atoms were still a rumor, so direction was anyone’s guess. Benjamin Franklin made the call, flow leaves $+$ terminal, returns to $-$ terminal going through the closed circuit. Every arrow, rule and diagram engineers use today was built on his guess, conventional current.

A century and a half later the electron was made visible, and it was negative, drifting the other way, pushed out of the $-$ terminal back to the $+$ terminal. That is, electrons start by pushing out of $-$ terminal where there is extra charge/electrons, around the loop and pulled into the $+$ terminal. The guess was backwards. Books were not reprinted, both arrows describe same loop moving same energy. Only bookkeeping points the other way.

Even a meter can’t take sides, 0.30 A reads as 0.30 whichever arrow you draw. Engineers write $+$ to $-$ and electrons march $-$ to $+$ and now you know both.

# No Loop no Light
A river can flow downhill forever, but charges in motion can’t just flow away, wire would run out of that sea of charge. Only one shape works, a loop. Must be a fully closed path that includes the voltage source. Every circuit has a battery holding potential difference, and components waiting to access, without that closed path, nothing happens the voltage source can’t push the charge into motion. A gap anywhere in the loop prevents charge from moving through components (ie. R/L/C) even if the break is nowhere near them. So a series loop is alive or not as *ONE* thing, no such place as far away in a circuit. One break anywhere in a series stops flow everywhere.

In order of how switch closes and activates a lamp’s circuit:

- Switch closes final gap
- Push of voltage/pot. diff. sweeps entire circuit
- Sea of charge flows through the lamp
- Lamp glows

So also note that a battery holds its voltage while zero current flows, means that potential difference can wait without a path until whenever there is a complete closed path.
